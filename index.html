Work I can walk you through

Two projects built the way the work is done in practice, with the workpapers and reasoning published in full.

SOC 2 Type II examination, from the auditor's side

I spend my days on the audited side of SOC 2, so I built an examination from the other side of the table. For a fictional SaaS company, I scoped Security, Availability, and Confidentiality, built a 19-control matrix mapped to the Trust Services Criteria, tested populations for completeness, sampled 52 items across access and change management, and evaluated 4 exceptions for root cause and opinion impact.

What I learned: an access review that ran on time was still an exception, because the account it flagged was never removed. A control being performed isn't the same as a control working.

Read the workpapers

AI vendor risk assessment of two AI meeting assistants

A third-party risk review of two AI note-takers for a sales team handling confidential customer data. I rated inherent risk across six factors, designed a 28-question AI due diligence questionnaire mapped to ISO/IEC 42001, the NIST AI RMF, and the EU AI Act, assessed both vendors from their public documentation, and recommended one for approval with conditions and the other for escalation.

What I learned: one vendor's promise not to train on customer data covered generative models only. In vendor risk, the exact wording of a commitment is where the risk hides.
